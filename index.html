What is Pickax?

Pickax is an algorithm-free social media platform founded by Jeff Dornik, with algorithm-free feeds. Pickax — sometimes called Pickax Social — supports short posts, long-form articles, and Rumble videos right in the feed. Visit Pickax.

How to turn a Pickax post into an image

To turn a Pickax post into an image, use Pickax2Image:

  1. Copy the Pickax post link (it looks like https://pickax.com/post/######).
  2. Paste it into the tool above and press Generate Image.
  3. Preview the result and download the finished PNG.

Pickax2Image is a free Pickax post to image converter. The image is built in your browser from the post's actual content — no screenshot needed, no sign-up. It works with Pickax posts only, not posts from other social networks.
