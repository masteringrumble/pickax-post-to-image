Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 25, 2026

1. The short version

Pickax Post to Image (pickax2image.top) is a free tool that turns public Pickax posts into downloadable PNG images. We don't have user accounts, we don't ask for your name or email, and the image itself is built inside your browser — the post content you work with is never uploaded to us or stored anywhere.

2. What we collect

  • Post URLs you enter. When you paste a Pickax post link, our server (a Cloudflare Worker) fetches that public post page to extract the post data. The URL is processed in memory to fulfill your request and is not stored in a database or tied to you.
  • Post images. If your browser can't load a post's photos or profile pictures directly, our worker re-serves them so the image can be built. These are public images from the post itself — Cloudflare may keep copies in its edge cache for up to 7 days for performance. They are never tied to you.
  • Analytics. We use Google Analytics 4 to understand aggregate usage (e.g. how many people visit, which country they're in, what device they use). It does not tell us who you are, and we exclude our own visits. Analytics only runs if you accept cookies in the consent banner.
  • Security data. Our host Cloudflare may process technical data (like IP address) to protect the site from abuse and attacks. This is standard for virtually every site behind Cloudflare.

3. What we never collect

  • No accounts, no passwords, no names, no email addresses.
  • The PNG image is rendered on a canvas in your browser — your generated images never leave your device.
  • We don't buy, sell, or share personal data with data brokers. There is nothing to sell.

4. Cookies

We only set non-essential cookies if you click Accept in the cookie banner. You can change your mind anytime by clearing your browser's site data for pickax2image.top — the banner will appear again on your next visit.

6. Your rights (GDPR / UK GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area or the UK, you have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the personal data we hold about you, to receive it in a portable format, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. In practice there is almost nothing to provide — we hold no accounts or profiles — but write to us and we will respond.

Our lawful bases: consent for analytics and third-party widgets (via the cookie banner), and legitimate interests for security processing and for fetching the public post page you asked us to convert.

7. California (CCPA/CPRA)

We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by California law, and we do not use sensitive personal information. California residents may request to know, delete, or correct personal information using the contact below.

8. Children

This tool is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
